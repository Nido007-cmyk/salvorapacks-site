The guides in our products are general educational information. They summarize public guidance published by official sources, cited inside each guide. They are not medical advice, they are not a diagnosis, and they are not a substitute for professional help, proper training or your local emergency services.

Where the guidance comes from. SALVORA PACKS does not write its own emergency or health advice. Every guide compiles public guidance published by official sources, such as FEMA, Ready.gov, the CDC and the American Red Cross, and cites those sources inside the guide. Before a guide is sold, we check it line by line against the sources it cites. What we add is the work around the guidance: choosing it, organizing it, putting it in plain language, translating it and laying it out so it can be found fast and used offline. Choosing, summarizing and translating are editorial work, and no check catches everything. If a guide and its official source ever disagree, the source is right and the guide is wrong: follow the source, and tell us so we can fix the guide.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number first. Use a guide only if it helps after that. Alerts and orders from your local authority always come before any guide we sell.

First aid and health guidance change over time. Each guide carries the date it was verified against its sources. If a guide and a professional ever disagree, follow the professional.

Our binders, planners and templates are organizers. They help you keep records in order. They are not legal, tax or financial advice, they are not legal documents, they are not medical records issued by anyone, and filling one in does not create any official document. For a decision about a divorce, custody, taxes, a lease, a business or your money, ask a professional licensed where you live.

The Pets pack is general information too. It does not replace your veterinarian or an emergency animal clinic.
