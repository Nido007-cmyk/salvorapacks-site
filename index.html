Questions · Preguntas

Before you ask

Do I need the NIDO app to use a pack?

No. Every pack brings its complete readable edition, so you open the files and read. NIDO is just a comfortable way to read them on your phone.

No: cada pack trae su edición legible completa. NIDO es solo una forma cómoda de leerlos en el teléfono.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download. Once the files are on your device, everything works with no connection. That's why we made Salvora in the first place.

Solo para descargar. Después, todo funciona sin conexión.

What exactly do I receive?

You get files, and Gumroad delivers them at checkout. Guides, plus audio on the audiobooks. You download them once and keep them. There's no DRM, you need no account, and nothing turns into a subscription. They're yours.

Archivos entregados por Gumroad: se descargan una vez y son tuyos, sin DRM ni suscripciones.

Which languages are included?

English, Spanish and Portuguese across the store. The one exception is the Annual Papers & Taxes Binder, which comes in English and Spanish only. Its card says so.

Inglés, español y portugués. La única excepción es la Carpeta anual de papeles e impuestos: solo inglés y español.

What if a file doesn’t work?

Write to soporte@salvorapacks.com. If a file is defective, we repair it or replace it with a working copy at no cost. If you bought the same thing twice by accident, we refund the duplicate. The full policy is in the footer.

Escríbenos a soporte@salvorapacks.com: el archivo se repara o se cambia sin coste, y la compra duplicada por accidente se reembolsa.

Important: in a real emergency, call your local emergency number first. Our guides inform you and help you prepare. They don't replace the judgment of the professionals on the scene: doctors, firefighters and the authorities.

Importante: en una emergencia real, llama primero a tu número local de emergencias. Estas guías informan y preparan. No sustituyen el criterio de los profesionales que están en el lugar: médicos, bomberos y autoridades.
