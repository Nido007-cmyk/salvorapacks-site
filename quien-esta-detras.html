SALVORA PACKS is a small publishing project run by one person: Aldo Rodriguez, in Tucson, Arizona. There is no team behind a curtain and no office. The shop sells digital products: packs of emergency and home guides, fillable binders and templates, books and audio editions, in English and Spanish.

Every product starts from official public sources: the guidance of agencies and organizations such as FEMA, the Red Cross, the CDC and Ready.gov, cited inside each guide. Production is done with AI assistance, and that is declared on every product listing on Gumroad and Etsy. What makes the work ours is the checking: each guide is verified line by line against the sources it cites before it is sold. If a guide and its official source ever disagree, the source is right and the guide is wrong: follow the source, and tell us. An error found after publication is corrected, and buyers can download the corrected copy from the platform where they bought. When the correction matters for safety, we also write to them.

Our guide packs carry signed manifests and a VERIFICACION file with the published fingerprints of their files, so a buyer can check that a copy is whole and untampered. Nothing we sell needs an account with us or a subscription, and no pack needs our app: the readable edition of every pack opens with free tools.

Questions, corrections and problems with a file: soporte@salvorapacks.com. Our aim is to answer within two business days.
